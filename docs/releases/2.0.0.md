## English

Hope 2.0.0 is a rewrite: the Electron app is gone, this is a native Tauri 2 app with a SQLite file of your own, desktop cards, a menu bar timer, sync, and iPhone / Apple Watch companions that are not released yet.

**Downloads (unsigned):**

- macOS: `Hope_2.0.0_aarch64.dmg` (Apple silicon) or `Hope_2.0.0_x64.dmg` (Intel). First launch: right-click `Hope.app` → **Open**.
- Windows: `Hope_2.0.0_x64-setup.exe`. On the SmartScreen dialog click **More info → Run anyway**.

**Not in this release:** the iPhone and Apple Watch apps. They exist and run, but are not on the App Store yet; the front end and back end are still being developed. Android is not planned unless requested. See the [README](https://github.com/dan9574/hope-time-tracker#readme) for what the mobile apps look like and what comes next.

**What you get on the desktop**

- Today as a ring from wake-up to bedtime, with the timeline underneath; this week and this month.
- Activities with sub-activities, eight colours; editable records; a journal per day.
- Schedule with one-off and recurring plans, auto-logging, and `.ics` timetable import (class time stays out of your totals unless you switch it on).
- Desktop cards (now / today / this week) under your desktop icons, with real blur on macOS and Windows.
- Menu bar / tray timer. Light, dark, tints, accent colour, ring width.
- JSON export and import. Optional sync: sign up in Settings → Sync and the same data follows you to another computer.
- A break reminder every 90 minutes of running time (system notification).

**Known limits**

- The installers are not code-signed or notarized yet.
- Windows: the desktop cards use per-card acrylic; tested on one Windows 11 machine.

## 中文

Hope 2.0.0 是一次重写：Electron 版没有了，这是原生的 Tauri 2 app，数据是你自己的 SQLite 文件，有桌面卡片、菜单栏计时、同步，以及尚未发布的 iPhone / Apple Watch 伴侣 app。

**下载（未签名）：**

- macOS：`Hope_2.0.0_aarch64.dmg`（Apple 芯片）或 `Hope_2.0.0_x64.dmg`（Intel）。第一次打开：右键 `Hope.app` → **打开**。
- Windows：`Hope_2.0.0_x64-setup.exe`。SmartScreen 提示里点 **更多信息 → 仍要运行**。

**本次不包含：** iPhone 和 Apple Watch 版。它们已经做出来、能跑，但还没上 App Store，前端和后端也还在继续开发。Android 暂不支持，除非有人提需求。手机 app 长什么样、接下来做什么，见 [中文说明](https://github.com/dan9574/hope-time-tracker/blob/main/README.zh-CN.md)。

**桌面版有什么**

- 今天：从起床到睡觉的一个环，下面是时间线；本周、本月。
- 活动与子活动，八种颜色；记录可编辑；每天一条日记。
- 日程：单次和周期安排、自动计入、`.ics` 课表导入（上课时间默认不计入总时长，可在设置里打开）。
- 桌面卡片（现在 / 今天 / 本周）贴在桌面图标下，macOS 和 Windows 都有真实毛玻璃。
- 菜单栏 / 托盘计时。浅色、深色、色调、强调色、环的粗细。
- JSON 导出导入。可选同步：设置 → 同步里注册账号，另一台电脑登录即得到同一份数据。
- 连续计时每满 90 分钟的休息提醒（系统通知）。

**已知限制**

- 安装包尚未签名、公证。
- Windows：桌面卡片用逐卡 Acrylic，只在一台 Windows 11 上测过。
